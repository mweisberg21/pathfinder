Set up Pathfinder.

  1. Open the download and drag Pathfinder to Applications.
  2. Launch Pathfinder. Choose Enable Insertion, turn on Pathfinder in the macOS permission pane, and return to the app. You can also use Copy without that permission.
  3. Press Control–Option–Space. Change your shortcut anytime in Settings.

A few good questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. Free to download and use, with no account, subscription, or paid tier.

Does it upload my files?

No. Pathfinder inserts a plain-text path. It does not attach the file or give an AI app access to it.

What stays on my Mac?

Your pins, preferences, and usage history stay locally on your Mac. Pathfinder has no analytics or file uploads and does not store your chat text. You can turn off usage history or clear it in Settings.

Search uses Spotlight. Excluded or unindexed files may not appear; use the app’s file chooser to select them directly. Read the privacy details.

Which Macs does it support?

This download is for Apple silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later. Intel Macs are not supported by this build. Version 0.1.0 is an early release, signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

Path insertion has been tested with ChatGPT and Claude. Other text fields may behave differently; Copy is always available. A fresh installation on another Mac remains to be tested.
